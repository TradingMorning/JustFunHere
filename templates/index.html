What is it

A single dashboard for every platform you post to

If you make video content, you already know the tax: export a clip, open YouTube, upload, write a title. Switch tabs. Open Instagram, upload again, re-crop, re-caption. FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is Falcon Spido?

A tool that lets you connect your own social media accounts and upload, schedule, or publish your own video content to them — YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, and Telegram — from one dashboard, instead of doing it manually on each app.

Who is this for?

Creators, small teams, and agencies who publish the same video content across more than one platform and want to stop doing it manually, one app at a time.

Is my data and are my accounts safe?

Yes. We connect to each platform using their own official login — we never see or store your platform passwords. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I disconnect a platform or delete my data?

Yes, any time, in one click — see Data Deletion Instructions for every way to do it.

Do I need a Business account for Instagram?

Yes — Instagram's own API requires the account be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Personal Instagram accounts can't be connected, due to Instagram's own platform rules.

What happens if a scheduled post fails?

It never fails silently — your dashboard shows the exact reason (for example, a platform-side content rule, a missing permission, or a temporary outage on that platform's end), so you always know what happened.
